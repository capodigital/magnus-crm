# Ilustraciones de acceso de Magnus CRM

Creación: 16 de noviembre de 2026, con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Dos ilustraciones originales con transparencia, optimizadas a WebP de 1200 px conservando el canal alfa. Los originales generados permanecen en el almacenamiento local de Codex; las imágenes utilizadas por la aplicación están dentro del repositorio.

- Login: `public/images/illustrations/auth/magnus-login.webp` — relaciones entre clientes, conversaciones y seguimiento.
- Registro: `public/images/illustrations/auth/magnus-register.webp` — construcción de un espacio de trabajo y avance de oportunidades.
- Integración: `src/components/auth/AuthPageLayout.tsx`, compartida por login y registro. El logotipo y los textos siguen siendo elementos nativos de la interfaz. Las imágenes son decorativas y no sustituyen contenido ni controles.
- En anchos inferiores a 900 px se oculta el panel y el elemento picture selecciona un píxel transparente local, evitando descargar las ilustraciones. Ambos modos de color usan los mismos recortes transparentes.

## Prompt final: login

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a bespoke editorial 3D illustration for the left panel of Magnus CRM authentication pages, NOT a UI screenshot. Style: refined architectural product still life, carefully crafted satin ceramic, softly frosted glass and brushed brass, precision bevels, warm studio daylight, generous negative space, restrained and calm. Exact brand palette: deep petroleum teal #0F766E, ink navy #10212A, pale mint #7FD7C6, small muted gold #C89B3C details, ivory ceramic. Square composition; the whole subject centered and entirely within the middle 80% of the canvas, with ample clear margins all around. Background must be genuinely transparent, including around soft contact shadows. No people, no mascot, no screens, no letters, no numerals, no text, no labels, no watermarks, no neon, no purple. Commercial quality, exceptionally clean geometry and realistic materials. Scene: a bespoke sculptural relationship hub. A broad satin teal continuous ribbon folds into a low, architectural double arch reminiscent of an abstract M, positioned on a thin floating ivory rounded platform. Three colored connection nodes (ink navy, mint, warm gold) sit at key points of the ribbon, evoking connected client relationships. Around it, only three restrained small sculptural objects: a frosted chat bubble with two inset dots, a minimal ivory contact tile bearing an abstract profile silhouette, and a little teal checkmark medallion. Slender precision brass connector lines link these objects into the central structure. Isometric three-quarter view, tactile, sophisticated, substantial rather than toy-like. Keep a clear visual hierarchy and spacious composition.
